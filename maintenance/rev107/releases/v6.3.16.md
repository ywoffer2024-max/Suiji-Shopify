# SUIJI v6.3.16

The Shop All picture menu used the collar as its Necklace cover and left the fifth category without a photograph. The Necklace cover now uses the reviewed CROSS-S chain. The fifth entry keeps its Bracelets & bangles / Bracelets name and existing destination, with the collar photograph explicitly selected by the merchant. Desktop and mobile share this mapping. The change updates two Liquid templates and the synchronized DESIGN contract. It does not change product classification, collection membership, prices, availability, layout, fonts or controls.
