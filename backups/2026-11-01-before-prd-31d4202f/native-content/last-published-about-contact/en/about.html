Jewelry that changes with you.

Since 2014, our work in handmade jewelry has been shaped by conversations with the people who wear it. SUIJI grew from a wish we heard often: to keep a design you love and find more ways to wear it.

What SUIJI means

The name SUIJI expresses the idea of following yourself. Your style can change from one day to the next while still feeling like you. We make room for that choice in the way our jewelry comes together.

Why structure

In our custom work, some customers wanted a favorite charm to appear in matching earrings, a ring or a necklace. Others wanted to change a detail for a different outfit or occasion. Buying a whole new piece each time often meant buying the same parts again.

That experience led us to a modular approach: keep the base you love, choose another compatible charm, or carry a favorite detail across different clasp-equipped bases. The pieces can work together without making you start again with every new idea.

Core-Lock & Modular System

Bases give the jewelry its form and wearing position. Charms bring colour, texture and personal meaning. Core-Lock is the SUIJI multi-use clasp that connects compatible elements.

Every SUIJI charm fits bases equipped with the SUIJI multi-use clasp. Bare hoops and other components may need an additional attachment, so each listing explains what is supplied and what else is needed. A charm moves from one position to another; it does not become several charms at once.

During the later development of Core-Lock, French engineers specializing in springs contributed to refining the spring structure, mechanics and the pressure needed to operate the clasp.

The designer

Chunyu Wen, SUIJI’s founder and Brand & Product Lead, brings her background in bespoke fine jewelry to the brand’s design, structural development and product direction. She studied Visual Communication at Tsinghua University’s Academy of Arts & Design and Brand Management at INSEEC in France.

Her work begins with the relationship between a decorative element and the structure that makes it wearable. Form, proportion and the experience of wearing the complete piece matter as much as how it looks on display.

The studio

Chunyu leads design and product development, with Yue Wang bringing the collections, stories and practical information together. Handcraft and structural design meet in pieces that leave room for your own choices.

The conversations that began in custom jewelry still guide us: how someone wants to wear a piece, which details they want to keep, and what they would like to change. Personal expression remains the starting point.

Materials and continued use

We use titanium alloy for the parts designed to sit against the skin. Other materials are chosen to suit the design and the way each piece is made, with lightness guiding every choice. We consider how the complete piece feels on the body, as well as how it looks.

We use samples to refine the proportions of metal around each stone and confirm how it is set. Surface finishes are chosen to suit the shape of each piece.

Our approach to sustainability starts with keeping pieces in use: continue wearing a base you love, change a charm for another occasion and build from what you already own. We design for these choices so that a new look does not always mean a completely new piece.

A future shaped by you

We are working toward a future where everyone can wear jewelry they have had a hand in designing. SUIJI provides the structure; your ideas, choices and personal connections give each composition its character.

For now, begin with the available bases, charms and complete sets. Keep what feels like you, add what you need and let your collection take shape over time.
