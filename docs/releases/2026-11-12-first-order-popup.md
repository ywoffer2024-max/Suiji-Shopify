# First-order popup

Shopify Forms provides the first-order popup in the English and French storefronts. The popup opens ten seconds after a visitor lands on an eligible page. Customers enter their email address and see the 10% discount code after a successful submission. The form leaves email marketing consent as recorded on the customer profile.

The live theme has the Forms app embed enabled. Configure the popup and its translations in Shopify Forms. The discount is configured in Shopify Discounts.
